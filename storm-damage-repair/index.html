Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • In areas that see hurricanes, fit storm shutters or windows rated for impact
  • Know how to shut off your main water supply in case a storm damages a pipe
  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear so water drains properly in heavy rain
  • Before storm season each year, get the roof inspected and its problem areas repaired
  • Runoff should flow away from your foundation, so fix low spots and add downspout extensions
  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams

Signs It Is Time for Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Bubbling paint and soft, drooping spots overhead once the rain stops
  • Wind-driven rain coming in through windows, doors or wall penetrations
  • Damaged or displaced flashing around roof penetrations, valleys or edges
  • After a weather event, insulation inside walls or overhead feels soaked
  • New brown rings or damp marks on walls and ceilings once the storm passes
  • Standing water in attic spaces, especially by skylights and chimneys
  • From ground level, the roof shows shingles torn off, broken or raised
